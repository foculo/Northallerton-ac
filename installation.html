Installation

Planned for your property.
Installed with care.

A good installation starts with understanding the space and ends with you knowing how to use your system.

01 / Consultation

Understand the space

We’ll discuss what you want to achieve, the rooms involved and any existing equipment or constraints.

02 / System selection

Compare suitable options

System type and size depend on the room, layout and intended use. We install systems from a range of manufacturers. Our accreditations are with Panasonic for RAC and PACi systems and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries. We’ll explain which options suit the room, their availability and the proposal scope.

03 / Installation

Plan the practical details

We’ll agree equipment positions, pipework routes, access and the work to be completed before scheduling.

04 / Commissioning

Check the system

After installation, the system is checked and commissioned in line with the equipment and applicable requirements.

05 / Handover

Get comfortable using it

We’ll explain the controls and share information about ongoing care for the installed system.
