What we do

Air conditioning,
made straightforward.

From choosing a system to keeping it in good working order, get clear advice for your property and requirements.

01 / Installation

A system suited to your space.

We can discuss your rooms, how you use them and the installation options available. We’ll explain the proposed equipment and work before you decide how to proceed.

We install air conditioning from a range of manufacturers. Our installer accreditations are with Panasonic for RAC and PACi systems and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries. We’ll discuss other suitable equipment based on your needs and availability.

Ask about an installation →

02 / Servicing & maintenance

Look after your equipment.

Regular maintenance can help identify issues and keep equipment clean and operating as intended. The right schedule depends on the system and how it is used.

Contact us with the make and model, if known, and a short description of what you need.

Ask about servicing →

03 / Repairs & fault finding

Help when something changes.

If your system is not heating, cooling or operating as expected, get in touch. We’ll ask for the symptoms and equipment details to understand the next steps.

We’ll confirm availability and any inspection or repair charges before arranging work.
