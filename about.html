About Northallerton AC

Local service.
Careful work.

An independent air conditioning business based in Northallerton, supporting domestic and smaller commercial customers across North Yorkshire.

Our approach

Good advice is part of a good installation.

Every property has its own layout and priorities. We take time to understand how a room or premises is used, discuss practical equipment options and set out the work before it is agreed.

We install air conditioning from a range of manufacturers. Our installer accreditations are with Panasonic for RAC and PACi systems and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries; we’ll discuss equipment options based on the property, requirements and availability.

We aim to keep communication clear from the first enquiry through to system handover and ongoing maintenance.
